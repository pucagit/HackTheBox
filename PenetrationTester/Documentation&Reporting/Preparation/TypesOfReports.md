# Types of Reports
## Questions
1. Inlanefreight has contracted Elizabeth's firm to complete a type of assessment that is mostly automated where no exploitation is attempted. What kind of assessment is she going to be contracted for? **Answer: Vulnerability Assessment**
2. Nicolas is performing an external & internal penetration test for Inlanefreight. The client has only provided the company's name and a network connection onsite at their office and no additional detail. From what perspective is he performing the penetration test? **Answer: black box**
